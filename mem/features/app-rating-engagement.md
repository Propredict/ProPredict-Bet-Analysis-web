The +50 points reward for rating the app has been removed (no longer exists).

Rules:
- Never offer or mention any reward (points, gifts) for rating in the rating popup, rating card, or redirect step.
- Rating popup design otherwise stays the same: pre-filter with inline stars, Yes/Not really buttons, Google Play redirect for 4-5 stars, feedback for low stars, thanks step after successful submit.
- Popup appears ~40s on Android (after Daily Tips popup), uses global popup cooldown.
